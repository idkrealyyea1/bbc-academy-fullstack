About BBC English Academy

BBC English Academy helps students, professionals and young learners speak English with confidence — through speaking-first lessons, clear levels and teachers who know their students by name.

Chapter 01 — The first word

It starts with one brave sentence

Most students arrive afraid to speak. So the first lesson is a safe space: small groups, Arabic support when you need it, and speaking from the very first day — not after months of grammar.

Chapter 02 — The conversation club

English becomes something you enjoy

Weekly speaking practice on real-life topics, in small groups. You talk about travel, work and daily life — and slowly, the fear turns into fluency.

Chapter 03 — Feedback that moves you forward

You always know where you stand

Homework feedback, progress reports and a clear path from one level to the next. Whether it is IELTS preparation or everyday English, every step is measured — and celebrated.

Chapter 04 — English in real life

From the classroom to the meeting room

Meetings, emails, presentations and interviews in professional English — and, for younger learners, playful speaking-first classes. The goal was never just a certificate. It was a voice you can use.

Our mission

Make English usable, not just studied — so every student can speak, listen, read and write in real life.

Who we serve

Students, professionals and young learners — from Beginner (A1) to Advanced.

How we teach

Speaking-first, small groups, and a clear path from one level to the next.
