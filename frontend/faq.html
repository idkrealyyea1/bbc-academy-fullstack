Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know English before joining?

No — Beginner (A1) starts from zero. Take the placement test to find your level, or book a seat and the team will help you choose.

Are classes online or in person?

Classes are offered in person and online. Choose your format when you book, and your lesson link or classroom is confirmed with your seat.

How much does a course cost?

Fees depend on the level and the length of the course. Contact us for the current term's fees.

Do you offer certificates?

Yes — certificates are awarded on successful completion of a level.

How do I book?

Use the booking form on this site. The academy confirms your seat and level after you submit.
